Working capital and financing review

28 October 2026 · 16 quarters · INR crore · model arithmetic and assumptions review. These are planning scenarios, not verified funding requirements.

What changed

The former model called a working-capital target “60-day receivables” and applied 60/30 to monthly sales. The revised model separates the planning target from customer credit and states the annual base explicitly.

Working-capital target = annualised revenue × 60 / 365
Annualised revenue = final-month revenue × 12
Additional WC = closing WC target − modelled opening WC target
Cash used in a quarter = closing WC target − previous quarter’s WC target

60/365 = 16.4384% of annualised revenue. This is equivalent to 1.9726 months of revenue. The previous 200% was relative to monthly revenue, not annual revenue; it was not a twelvefold increase in capital.

Checks completed

All 16 quarters reconcile: capex and WC changes, cash sources and uses, interest, each plant-loan vintage, principal repayments, outstanding loans, dilution and exit equity value. Four borrowing modes were checked at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% coverage. Financial assumptions are distinguished from arithmetic consistency.

The financing selector sits above the seven-step chart. It updates the plan without replacing the dashboard’s existing presentation. Live screenshot access was blocked in this session; generated markup, event handlers and standalone SVG geometry were checked.
