# VC teardown: Tejas reading deck, slides 0–3

An independent reviewer read the slides as a senior partner at an Indian growth fund would. It checked every figure against the model's raw outputs and the founders' evidence notes (18 Nov 2026 screenshots).

## Verdict

**Biggest problem: the headlines contradict the model.** That costs the founders the high-IQ signal they are aiming for:
- Growth is "20%" on slides 1 and 3 and "~15%" on slide 2. The model has about 13% a month for the first two years and about 10% over four years.
- Slide 2's chart says −15% where its headline says −10%.
- "₹144 Cr by M14" is really ₹148 Cr in Q6 (ARR at month 14 is ₹88 Cr).
- "PBT" is really operating margin, before depreciation, interest and tax.
- Slide 1 calls margins "robust" while the business is losing 15%.

In a reading deck nobody is there to explain any of this. The first wrong number makes a partner audit every other one.

**Top 5 changes, in priority order:**
1. Rewrite every headline and subheadline from model figures only.
2. Rebuild slide 3 around **₹240 Cr (rounds 1–2) funding the plan, plus ₹2,250 Cr (rounds 3–4) for consolidation**, with no valuation leaks.
3. Split slides 0 and 1 into a **geography chain** and a **product chain** that both add up to the same ₹128 Cr a month in Q16. Give metros (49% of Q16 revenue) their own evidenced step.
4. Remove the mixed-unit charts: rupee dots plotted on a share axis (slide 0), and round dots on the ARR axis (slide 3).
5. Show the conservative choices: fixed-cost growth planned at 0.55 against 0.30 measured, influencer cost 10% against 5.5% actual, Bondure sized at 0.25× against a 0.5× market. Then remove the pills that duplicate evidence.

**What 60 seconds should leave a partner feeling:** "They know every city's numbers. They found one repeatable curve, modelled every new line and city off it, and set every assumption worse than what they've already measured. ₹240 Cr gets them to ₹128 Cr a month at a 16% margin, and they have the nerve to raise ₹2,250 Cr more to consolidate the region."

The current slides leave a different impression: "big hockey stick, inconsistent numbers, wants a lot of money."

## Slide 0: How it's built

**Five-second read:** the headline lands, then the eye goes to the chart. Nothing says which number matters.

**Logic.** The 30% is circular. Market size = tile sales ÷ 30%, then revenue = market size × 30%, which just gives back today's sales.

**Errors and problems:**
- The chart plots rupees on a share axis:
  - "Vidarbha · month 7 · ₹37.9 L" sits at about 37%, above the "30% today" ceiling, and at quarter 3 (month 9).
  - "Nagpur · month 4 · ₹20.4 L" sits at quarter 2 (month 6).
- "₹1.37 Cr a month" is per live *city*; a live dealer zone is ₹1.03 Cr.
- The large dealer zone (₹3 Cr, 15% of Q16 revenue) is missing.
- "Product size: 1× tile adhesive" tells the reader nothing; the real multipliers sit in small grey text.
- Arrow labels are clipped ("ecomes", "xtends to").

**What's correct:** the 8 → 30% curve, Raipur ₹2.04 Cr, "27% below", and ₹1.44 Cr + ₹0.16 Cr.

**Biggest assumption, smallest type:** "Large city ₹10 Cr" gets one line, but the 8 metros carry 49% of Q16 revenue (₹62.6 of ₹128 Cr).

**Founders' evidence to settle:** your notes call Raipur ₹50 L / Nagpur ₹40 L / Vidarbha ₹35 L / CG ₹15 L "markets", but they add up to tile *sales* (₹1.40 Cr against ₹1.44 Cr). If they were markets, Tejas would be selling 103% of its market.

**Pills.** Weak, because they don't attach to any step. "Hiring sets the pace: a city is mapped in under 2 months" gives a mapping fact to support a hiring claim. The hiring fact is "an RM takes ~5 months to reach full output; active RMs went from 5 to 14."

**Scores:** tribal knowledge 3 · ambition 3 · high IQ 3.

## Slide 1: What we sell

**Five-second read:** "20% MoM", then bars rising to 128. A partner checks it in his head: 1.8 → 128 over 45 months is about 10% a month. Trust is lost on the second slide.

**Errors:**
- "20% MoM": the model has ~13% a month to Q8 and ~10% over four years. History is 3.75× in 15 months, about 9% a month.
- "already robust margins": Q1 operating margin is −14.9%. Only CM1 (47%) and CM2 (20%) are robust.
- "₹144Cr ARR (2% PBT) by M14": M14 ARR is ₹88 Cr. ₹148 Cr arrives in Q6, at a 5.6% margin.
- "All 4 live markets at 30%": assumed, not observed.
- "Every new market follows the same curve": false for 64% of Q16 revenue, because large markets follow 3% → 21.3%.
- Lines not sold today are 69% of Q16 revenue, and uPVC (₹42.5 Cr) is bigger than tile (₹31.0 Cr). Yet uPVC and waterproofing are drawn in near-invisible pale blue.
- Build defects: a "Plot Area" tooltip was captured, the logo and footer are missing, and the nav has no tab for slide 0.

**Logic.** Deepen and replicate get equal weight. Deepening the live four adds ₹5.6 Cr; replication adds about ₹120 Cr, half of it from metros.

**Repetition.** It repeats slide 0: the curve, 84% repeat, and Raipur ₹2.04 Cr all appear twice.

**Scores:** tribal knowledge 1 · ambition 4 · high IQ 1.

## Slide 2: What we keep

**Five-second read:** the waterfall is the best visual in the deck. But the headline says −10% and the chart says −15%.

**What's correct:** the boxes (53 → 49, 27 → 21, 71× vs 29×, 34 → 14) and the bridge (+3.8, +6.7, +20.7 add up to 16.2%).

**Problems:**
- Cost of goods isn't a straight line down: it rises to 53.8% in Q8 as uPVC launches.
- Pills:
  - "Influencer payouts held at ~10%": July actual is 5.5%, so the plan is conservative, and the slide never says so.
  - "Best RMs already bring ₹38 L": cherry-picked; the typical mature RM brings ₹8–20 L.
  - "59% paid before dispatch": a working-capital fact that belongs on slide 3.
- "✓ PROVEN" in the boxes and "✓ already moving" on every bar say the same thing twice.

**Missing:** fixed-cost leverage supplies two-thirds of the gain (+21 of +31 points). Fixed cost was *measured* growing at 0.30× revenue's pace, and the plan uses 0.55×. That is the strongest high-IQ signal in the model, and it's not on the slide.

**Scores:** tribal knowledge 4 · ambition 2 · high IQ 3.

## Slide 3: What we need

**Five-second read:** "they want to raise a lot of money". "Capital magnet" sounds like raising for its own sake.

**Errors:**
- "₹100–1,000 Cr" matches nothing in the model: the peak need is ₹219 Cr, rounds 1–2 are ₹240 Cr, and all four rounds are ₹2,490 Cr.
- The subheadline "targeting next raise at ~₹1,500 Cr (6x current)" is a valuation, which breaks your no-valuations rule.
- Box A says "₹40 Cr buys plants, labs and working capital", but spending through Q5 is ₹52 Cr, and cash closes at −₹12 Cr in Q5 before round 2.
- The arrow verb "prices" hints at the pricing rule.
- The round dots don't sit at their values on the axis (round 1 sits near 100, not 40). The line between rounds implies a continuous quantity.
- ARR, an annual flow, and rounds, one-off cash, share one log axis. Bars on a log scale don't read as lengths.
- "repeat: rounds 3 and 4" says rounds 3–4 work like rounds 1–2, which contradicts "not needed for the plan".

**What's correct:** ARR ₹19 → ₹148 Cr (7.7×), round 2 ₹200 Cr, and ₹1 of capital → ₹3.6 of yearly revenue.

**Scores:** tribal knowledge 1 · ambition 4 (but reads as vanity) · high IQ 1.

## Reviewer's proposed structure

**Slide 0, Geography: "Where we sell"**
- **Evidence:** each live market's figures; Nagpur 8% in 3 months.
- **What we took:** live city ₹1.37 Cr and live zone ₹1.03 Cr at 100% share; the 8 → 30% curve.
- **How alike the next cities are:** medium cities 27% smaller; metros 10× bigger but on a lower 3 → 21% curve (adhesive reaches only 20–25% of tile jobs; metros are specifier-led).
- **How we scale:** 7 / 13 / 19 / 26 openings a year, paced by RMs (about 5 months to full output; 5 → 14 active).
- **Chart:** Q16 revenue by market class: live 4 ₹7.2 Cr, medium cities ₹24.3 Cr, large cities ₹62.6 Cr, medium zones ₹15.2 Cr, large zones ₹19.0 Cr.

**Slide 1, Product: "What we sell"**
- **Sizing:** each line's national market as a ratio to tile adhesive's ₹8,041 Cr: wood ₹8,572 Cr = 1×; windows ₹10,800 → ₹14,700 Cr = 1.72×; waterproofing ₹7,200 → ₹12,500 Cr = 1×.
- **Bondure set below its market:** 0.25× against a 0.5× market, at 60–80% gross margin; ~10% of sales in 3 months.
- **Same curve:** a new line sells through a relationship already won; 84% repeat.
- **When:** wood Q4 (+1 quarter per new market), uPVC Q6 (+2), waterproofing Q10 (+4).
- **Chart:** revenue by line with distinct colours. 69% of Q16 revenue comes from lines launched after today.

**Slide 2, CM1 / CM2**
- **CM1 = revenue − cost of goods:** 47% → 51%. Proof: freight 8.4% → 1.0%.
- **CM2 = CM1 − selling costs:** 20% → 30%. Proof: last-mile 10.2% → 6.3%; influencer planned at 10% against 5.5% actual.
- **Operating margin = CM2 − fixed costs:** −15% → +16%. Proof: revenue 3.75× vs fixed cost 1.49×; plan 0.55 vs measured 0.30.
- **Chart:** three lines (CM1, CM2, operating margin) over 16 quarters. The gaps between them are shaded and labelled "selling costs" and "fixed costs".

**Slide 3**
- Rounds 1–2 in one colour, rounds 3–4 in another.
- **Boxes:**
  - "₹240 Cr, two rounds"
  - "Funds the full organic plan"
  - "₹2,250 Cr more to consolidate regional brands and plants"
- **Bottom band:** "Rounds 3–4 · ₹2,250 Cr · mainly acquisitions of regional brands and plants; headroom to grow faster organically".
- **Rounds** as markers on the timeline, not on the ARR value axis.
